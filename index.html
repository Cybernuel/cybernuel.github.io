About

I'm Emmanuel. I make security make sense to the people it's meant to protect.

Nigerian-born and UK-based, I came to cybersecurity through the service desk: years of helping real people with real problems before I ever wrote a detection rule. That still shapes how I work.

I hold an MSc in Cybersecurity from Edinburgh Napier University and a BEng in Computer Engineering from the University of Ilorin. I've worked as a SOC Analyst, triaging alerts and building detections in Microsoft Sentinel and Defender, and I volunteer with The Cyber Helpline supporting victims of cybercrime.

Cybernuel is where I share it all: hands-on labs, plain-English explainers and a weekly newsletter.

Security · for everyone

5 habits that stop most hacks

  1. Use a password manager and a different password for every account.
  2. Turn on two-step verification, with an authenticator app rather than text codes where you can.
  3. Install updates on your phone and laptop as soon as they arrive.
  4. Pause before clicking. If a message is urgent, check it another way first.
  5. Back up the files you can't afford to lose.
